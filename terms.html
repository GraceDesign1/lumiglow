Terms & Conditions

Clear service expectations for LumiGlow Cleaning Services, from estimates and payment to safety and satisfaction.

1. Estimates

All estimates are based on the walkthrough or consultation and are valid for 14 days. Additional charges may apply if the property's condition differs significantly from the walkthrough. Approval will be obtained before additional work is performed.

2. Scope of Work

Only the services listed in the approved estimate and checklist are included. Additional requested services may result in additional charges.

3. Client Acceptance

By accepting the estimate via email, text message, or other written communication, the client agrees to the quoted price, scope of work, checklist, and these Terms & Conditions.

4. Payment

Payment is due upon completion unless otherwise agreed in writing. Accepted payment methods: Interac e-Transfer to lumiglowcleans@gmail.com and cash. Please include your name or property address with your e-Transfer.

5. Late Payments

Reminder after 7 days if payment has not been received. A second reminder will be issued if payment remains outstanding. If payment is still not received, LumiGlow reserves the right to refer the outstanding balance to a third-party collections agency or pursue other remedies available under applicable law.

6. Access to the Property

Clients are responsible for providing safe and timely access to the property.

7. Cancellation & Rescheduling

Please provide at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling.

8. Equipment & Supplies

LumiGlow provides professional cleaning supplies unless otherwise agreed. To help prevent the transfer of pet dander, allergens, and other contaminants between households, LumiGlow reserves the right to use the client's vacuum cleaner and/or other cleaning equipment when deemed necessary.

9. Pets

Pets should be secured or supervised during the cleaning appointment.

10. Satisfaction Guarantee

Notify us within 24 hours if you are dissatisfied. We will return to address missed areas within the agreed scope.

11. Existing Damage

LumiGlow is not responsible for pre-existing damage or normal wear and tear.

12. Health & Safety

We may refuse or discontinue service where conditions present a health or safety risk.

13. Photography

With your permission, before-and-after photos may be taken for documentation and will not be used publicly without written consent.

14. Limitation of Liability

Liability is limited to the value of the cleaning service provided.
